# PC.3 integrated slice and delayed A/B review preregistration

PC.3 renders the accepted PC.2 derivative as a fresh 288-frame, 640×360, 24 fps three-shot video using the same accepted camera routing and lighting. The original PB.6 video is A and the integrated modeling-plus-action result is B. Machine acceptance requires all frames, exact routing/identities, zero retained EXR, a decodable video, strong frame uniqueness/dynamics and measurable difference from A.

Machine PASS does not answer the aesthetic question. Only after the machine root is sealed will the owner review A, B and the six-panel contact sheet, then answer four fixed YES/NO questions about modeling detail, action intentionality, retained lighting/camera strengths and overall preference. The model may not answer for the owner.
