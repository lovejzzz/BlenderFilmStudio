# RC6 C15 preregistration — C14 transition order

C14 proved that raising the adaptive timestep ceiling from four to eight was a
large but incomplete repair. Its retained Mesh is coherent through frame 30,
crosses the one-percent cup-solid intrusion gate at frame 31, crosses source,
temporal and positive-body gates at frame 35, and expands catastrophically at
frame 36. This temporal order is informative but does not yet prove the same
late expansion exists in Data.

C15 therefore performs no Blender run, bake, save or render. It copies the
complete immutable 108-file C14 cache into one fresh bounded root, reads all 36
particle/velocity VDB metadata records and the saved configuration metadata,
and compares them with the retained C14 Mesh result and the independently
accepted C12/C13 Data result.

The classification is frozen before the copy exists. It asks whether cup-solid
intrusion precedes later correlated Data/Mesh expansion, whether Data and Mesh
expand without prior intrusion, whether only Mesh expands, or whether the order
remains inconclusive. Occupied sparse voxels are not liquid mass. The saved
terminal substep is not a count or complete history of solver steps.

C12–C14, the R40 Bullet trajectory, every fluid setting, Mesh setting and all
27 physical thresholds remain immutable. A passing diagnostic only selects the
layer for one later preregistered variable; it does not authorize a repair,
render or threshold change.
