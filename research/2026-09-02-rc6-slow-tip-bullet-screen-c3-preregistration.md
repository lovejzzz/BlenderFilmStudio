# RC6 slow-tip Bullet screen C3 preregistration

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: preregistered before attempt-50 root creation

C2 showed that a collision-only toe stop is discontinuous: one drive launched
the cup while three rebounded just below 45 degrees. C3 replaces that unstable
contact relationship with an explicit Blender Bullet hinge. The hinge connects
the active cup to a passive anchor below the floor, rotates only around world Y
and passes through the cup's measured lower-right tangent point.

The hinge is not animated or motorized. The unchanged visible actuator still
provides the force, and Bullet still owns every cup transform. At every frame
the experiment transforms the original hinge point through the evaluated cup
matrix and independently limits pivot drift to 5 mm. The exact mesh-surface,
45-degree, four-frame, floor, domain, subframe and authority gates remain
unchanged.

This is the final Bullet-only mechanism screen before any new moving-liquid
cost is admitted. A PASS selects a trajectory; it does not establish water or
cinematography.
