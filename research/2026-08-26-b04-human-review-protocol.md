# B04 blinded human review protocol v0.1

Date: 2026-08-26  
Status: frozen before collecting responses  
Scope: visible pickup quality only; reviewers must not see B04 metrics before submitting

## Review asset

- anonymized clip ID: `CLIP_A17F`
- source: the compiled B04 run-A scene, frames 1–144 at 24 fps;
- presentation: 960×540 SDR review video, fixed camera, motion blur disabled to expose discontinuities;
- audio: none;
- overlays: none;
- repetitions: reviewer must watch at least twice at normal speed.

The clip is a technical proxy, not a photoreal character. Reviewers judge whether the represented interaction is visually coherent, not whether the character design is attractive.

## Blinding

Before submitting, reviewers must not receive:

- machine check counts or pass/fail state;
- contact errors, overlap counts, clearance or transport measurements;
- first-run failure details;
- labels for approach, acquire, hold, release or retreat frames.

The collection page must contain only the clip, questions, response instructions and clip ID. The evidence page can be shown after the response file is saved.

## Questions

1. Approach naturalness: 1 (clear snap/teleport) to 5 (continuous and intentional).
2. Support readability: 1 (prop does not appear supported) to 5 (support relationship is visually clear).
3. Distracting hand/prop intersection: `YES`, `NO`, or `UNSURE`.
4. Visible acquire or release pop: `YES`, `NO`, or `UNSURE`.
5. Weight coherence: 1 (weightless/incoherent) to 5 (convincing for the depicted object).
6. Overall visual acceptance: `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `UNSURE`.
7. Optional free-text failure note, maximum 500 characters.

## Sampling and gate

This technical pilot requires at least three independent reviewers. It is not a population study.

B04 human review passes only when:

- at least three valid responses exist;
- median approach naturalness is at least 3;
- median support readability is at least 3;
- median weight coherence is at least 3;
- fewer than half of valid reviewers answer `YES` to distracting intersection;
- fewer than half answer `YES` to visible pop;
- a strict majority answer `PASS` overall.

`UNSURE` is preserved and is not silently converted to pass. Missing answers invalidate a response. Machine pass plus human fail remains B04 failure.

## Response record

Each response is a local JSON document containing:

- protocol version;
- clip ID;
- anonymous reviewer code supplied by the reviewer;
- UTC submission time generated by the browser;
- the seven answers;
- confirmation of two complete viewings;
- no name, email, IP address or remote submission.

The static research site downloads the response to the reviewer's computer. It does not transmit or aggregate responses. A later aggregation step must validate the protocol version, clip ID, answer completeness and unique reviewer code before computing the gate.

## Explicit limitations

- Reviewers may infer technical intent from the visibly simple proxy.
- The clip does not test photorealism, sound, edit rhythm or story context.
- A three-reviewer pilot cannot establish general audience preference.
- Disabling motion blur improves fault visibility but is not the final cinematic presentation.
