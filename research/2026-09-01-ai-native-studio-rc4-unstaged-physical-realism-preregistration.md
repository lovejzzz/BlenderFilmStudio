# RC4 unstaged physical realism preregistration

Date: 2026-09-01

RC3 established that the product can compile two different declarative action
graphs into Bullet-owned motion. RC4 keeps that mechanism and attacks the next
visible weakness: the basketball/bottle project still reads as a polished
prototype because the cause camera fits the entire floor, the environment has
few scale cues, bottle material separation is weak, and the effect still is a
peak-response frame rather than a derived rest state.

RC4 is one small real project, not another robot revision. A used regulation
basketball hits three differently filled household glass bottles on an aged
maple court. The product may add only reusable physical-look semantics and
physics-derived settled cinematography. It may not recognize this project,
author a result pose, type an observed contact/effect frame, animate the light,
or hide the collision with postprocess blur.

The same product bytes must retain the accepted RC3 D1 and H1 mechanisms. RC4
passes visually only if all ten preregistered questions are YES after all 48
contact frames are inspected, including an explicit comparison against RC3 H1.
Even a pass cannot claim liquid slosh, deformation, breakage, sound or general
photoreal filmmaking.
