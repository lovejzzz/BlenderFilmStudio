# RC6 C12 — real-impact liquid Preview preregistration

## Question

Can the exact retained R40 Bullet basketball impact drive a measurable, solver-owned APIC liquid spill during the already admitted frame 1–36 causal window, without losing the conservation, topology and containment quality learned in attempt-70?

This is the first integrated real-impact liquid gate. It is deliberately not a render or a film-quality judgment. If it fails, the failure remains evidence and the next correction must address the measured cause rather than make the liquid look fuller by eye.

## Frozen causal construction

- Start from the exact accepted static-liquid source blend.
- Recreate R40, not by copying cup or basketball poses, but by the same physical cause: four linear striker keys, an unkeyed active basketball, an unkeyed active cup with an explicit 2 mm Bullet margin, and the 0.30 m by 0.04 m passive ramp.
- Bake Bullet once and compare all 36 cup and ball samples with the retained R40 evidence before any liquid bake.
- Preserve the accepted attempt-70 Preview-96 APIC settings, including particle radius 1.8, particle band width 4.0, fractional distance 0.25, two minimum timesteps, and mesh reconstruction settings.
- Derive eight cup-effector subframes from the measured 72.10387 mm maximum cup-surface motion and the 9.375 mm Preview voxel.
- Treat the floor and passive ramp as explicit static fluid effectors. The ramp must exclude fluid physically; it cannot merely be visible or Bullet-only geometry.
- Bake Data once and Mesh once in the same Blender process. Do not save or render.

## Frozen physical acceptance

The integrated solve must remain R40-exact before and after Mantaflow. Contact must derive at frame 19 and the first 70-degree sample at frame 36. Cache output must contain exactly 108 files—one config, Data VDB and Mesh object per frame.

Every frame must have a liquid mesh. Source-relative volume error may not exceed 25%, temporal drift may not exceed 15%, positive liquid bodies must remain within 1–16, connected components within 32, the largest component must retain at least half the vertices, and aggregate topology must remain manifold.

Before contact, less than 5% of liquid vertices may be exterior to the cup interior plus one Preview voxel. After contact, a significant spill must be derived from the first exterior fraction at or above 5%, and at least 5% must remain exterior at frame 36. Cup-local centroid motion must reach at least 25 mm so a nominal boundary crossing cannot pass as the only response.

World-floor, modeled cup-solid and passive-ramp deep intrusion are each capped at 1%. No liquid vertex may leave the domain after a one-voxel inset. These solid and domain tests are new consequences of combining the real ramp with the liquid solve; none are relaxed from an observed result.

## Evidence and stop rule

The experiment uses exactly one existing verified binary start, one Bullet bake, one Data bake and one Mesh bake. It allows no native build, render, product save, network call, engine source edit or engine remote write. The fresh work root is capped at 2 GiB and the research evidence root at 64 MiB, with at least 100 GiB reserve plus the projected 2 GiB before admission.

Run only attempt-84. Preserve a machine PASS, scientific FAIL or harness failure unchanged. A successful result proves only one 36-frame Preview-tier same-solve impact/liquid event; it does not establish the cup's landing, long-term spill persistence, Final-192 behavior, rendered appearance, cinematic quality, deformation or general liquid competence.
