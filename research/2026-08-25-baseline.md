# Technical feasibility baseline — 2026-08-25

## Research question

Can AI translate directorial intent into editable, verifiable and repeatable Blender scenes that render to cinema-grade masters?

## Short answer

**Partly.** The deterministic half of the pipeline is ready: scene assembly from structured data, exact cameras, authored lighting, simulation with prepared setups, Cycles path tracing, OpenEXR/AOV output and color-managed compositing. The general content-creation half remains discontinuous: hero-character creation, deformation-ready topology, cinematic micro-performance, complex contact, inverse lighting/material recovery and fully automatic long-form artistic direction.

## Engineering decision

Do not let an LLM write the final `.blend` state directly. Introduce a restricted `SceneSpec` contract, validate it, compile it deterministically through Blender's Python API, and retain pure-CG EXR/AOV output as ground truth. Any neural realism pass must be optional, maskable and compared against that ground truth.

## Proposed first experiment

One fixed character, one fixed interior, a six-second dolly-in, one prop contact and one focus transition. Acceptance criteria: deterministic rebuild, local editability, automated technical checks and delivery of EXR/AOV/preview/report artifacts.
