# RC2 physical-light transfer preregistration

RC2 is the first deliberately simple unseen transfer after the robot capstone.
The project is a short physical story, *The Signal Gate*: a grooved ceramic
sphere rolls down a metric ramp under gravity, hits a hinged occluding shutter,
and opens a path for a static warm light to reveal a relief signal.

The simplifying choice is scientific, not aesthetic. A sphere, ramp, hinge and
light gate have unambiguous causal relationships. Blender Bullet must own the
sphere motion and the shutter outcome. The lamp power and color remain exact
and unanimated. The visible illumination may change only because the simulated
shutter geometry changes the aperture.

The product must consume one strict declarative schema. It may create modeled
ramp rails, aperture framing, hinge hardware, shutter ribs, sphere grooves and
a relief receiver, but it may not branch on this project id or fixture hash.
Initial conditions and cinematic intent are authored; contact time, trajectory,
rotation, shutter angle, opening, rebound/settling and final poses are measured
solver results.

Acceptance combines physical receipts, a closed-shutter counterfactual render,
pixel luminance measurement, three semantic stills, a 48-frame contact clip and
save/reopen comparison of actual simulated transforms. All nine direct visual
questions must be YES. Motion blur, glow, haze or camera polish cannot conceal
a weak primary solve.

The 2026-09-01 read-only generic F0 host check reports 130 GiB free against its
conservative 160 GiB clean-build requirement. RC2 preregistration and
accepted-binary development may proceed, but no RC2 native source build begins
until admission passes. No historical evidence will be deleted to manufacture
that pass.
