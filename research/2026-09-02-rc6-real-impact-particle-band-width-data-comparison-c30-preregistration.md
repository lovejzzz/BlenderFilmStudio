# RC6 C30 preregistration — Signed Data/Mesh loss after band narrowing

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: frozen before attempt-109 root creation

C30 performs no Blender operation. It copies all 108 files from immutable C29
into a fresh bounded root, then uses the accepted engine Python/OpenVDB runtime
to measure every frame's particle occupied support, velocity occupied support
and reconstructed Mesh volume against coherent frame 22. C18's audited C19
samples remain the comparison.

Unlike the earlier expansion-only comparisons, C30 freezes a signed 15% loss
classifier before reading the copy. It records the first velocity, particle and
Mesh loss frames separately, their minimum drifts, their correlations, and the
existing positive-expansion measurements. Occupied sparse support is not exact
mass, so even a same-frame response may localize the layer without identifying
one Mantaflow operation.

The only allowed outcomes are: particle and Mesh loss both observed, Mesh loss
without particle-support loss, particle-support loss without Mesh loss, or no
15% Data/Mesh loss. The result may not repair or reinterpret C29, authorize a
universal/adaptive band-width policy, select another physical value, render or
mutate any retained evidence.
